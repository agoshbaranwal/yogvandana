Questions people ask

I have never done yoga. Can I join?
Yes. Both batches take beginners. Tell Vandana at the trial and she adjusts the class for you.
I have back pain. Is this safe for me?
Tell Vandana at the trial. She adapts the practice and says honestly if a class is not right for you. Yoga supports care from your doctor; it does not replace it.
What do I need at home?
A mat, a little floor space, and a phone or laptop for Zoom. Nothing else.
