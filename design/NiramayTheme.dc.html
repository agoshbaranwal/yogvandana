Direction 4 · the theme
निरामय · free from disease
The site is organised around the visitor's illness, not around the teacher. A person arrives with a problem, picks it on the first screen, and everything after that speaks to that problem: what yoga does for it, the daily slip of practice, students who had the same thing, and the batch to join. The teacher's credentials are the reason to trust, placed inside every ailment page. The tone is a yoga shivir's confidence with a clinic's order.
Why this: nine in ten Indians already believe yoga prevents or manages disease, and only about one in ten practises. The site's job is not to convince people that yoga works; it is to remove the reasons they have not started: no teacher, no fixed time, not sure it is for their condition.
योग वंदना
सर्वे सन्तु निरामयाः
"May all be free from disease." The line from the shanti mantra is the site's motto. A vandana is a prayer; this is the prayer the brand name promises. It sits under the logo in the footer and opens the About page.

Four families of ailments, four colours

Bhagwa stays the brand: buttons, marks, the claim. Each family of ailments gets one colour, used only on its tiles, chips and slip, so a visitor with knee pain always sees madder, a visitor with sugar always sees peacock blue. Pink and blue survive from the last round, with jobs. Kohl takes the footer.

The slip: योग की पर्ची

Every ailment page carries one. It is the shape every Indian knows from a doctor's pad, used for a daily practice: the practices, the minutes, the batch, when to review. Her name and degree at the top do the credibility work. A design device, not a medical document.
