# How this site talks

Agosh set this by rewriting six lines himself (4 Sep 2026). Everything below is
read off those six. When new copy is written, it is written to this.

| He replaced | With |
|---|---|
| आपको कौन सी बीमारी है? | किस बीमारी का हल चाहिए? |
| इन्हें फ़र्क पड़ा | स्वास्थ्य की ओर |
| पहले बात कीजिए। पहली बात मुफ़्त। | कॉल पर अपनी तकलीफ़ बताइए। योग और प्राणायाम द्वारा हल बताया जाएगा। |
| योग से हर बीमारी ठीक हो सकती है। | योग एवं प्राणायाम से अपनी बीमारियों का हल पाइए। |
| रोज़ सुबह या शाम, वंदना जी के सामने, अपने घर से। छोटा ग्रुप, ज़ूम पर। दवा डॉक्टर की सलाह से ही घटती है। | ऑनलाइन ग्रुप क्लास · कॉल पर सलाह · 1:1 क्लास |

## The six rules that follow from it

**1. हल is the word.** The reader has come for a remedy, not a diagnosis. Ask
what they want solved — "किस बीमारी का हल चाहिए?" — never what is wrong with
them. Every claim on the site offers a हल; credibility rule 7 fails the build
if one stops doing so.

**2. Invite, do not assert.** पाइए, बताइए, चुनिए, पूछिए. The old headline
announced a fact about yoga; the new one asks the reader to go and get
something. A sentence the reader can act on beats a sentence they can only
agree or disagree with.

**3. Name the method: योग एवं प्राणायाम.** These two words are the credential
this audience recognises, so say them. This is the *category* only — which
asana, which pranayama, and at what time stay behind the consultation, and
credibility rule 6b fails the build if a named practice appears anywhere on the
site.

**4. List the offer as nouns.** "ऑनलाइन ग्रुप क्लास · कॉल पर सलाह · 1:1 क्लास",
not three sentences describing what it feels like. A reader deciding whether to
call wants to know what is on offer, not the atmosphere.

**5. Face forward.** "स्वास्थ्य की ओर", not "इन्हें फ़र्क पड़ा". Name where the
reader is going, not what happened to somebody else.

**6. Price and caveats are small print, never a headline.** The band used to
lead with "पहली बात मुफ़्त"; it now leads with what happens on the call. The
free first call, the ₹200 consultation and the ₹1,000 batch still appear on
every page before a payment — rule 6c enforces the consultation price — but as
a line under the ask, not as the ask.

## Two things this changed about earlier decisions

**Everyday loanwords are in.** क्लास, कॉल, ऑनलाइन, ग्रुप, 1:1 — all five of his
rewrites use them. An earlier pass had stripped loanwords out; that was wrong
for an audience that says "क्लास", not "कक्षा".

**Buttons still say only two things.** "वंदना जी से बात करें" and "जुड़ें और
भुगतान करें" (rule 19). पूछिए and बताइए belong in headings, leads and text
links. A first pass at this round put "{x} का हल पूछिए" on twenty-four buttons;
the rule caught it, and it was right to.

## The result card

A result is read as a record, not as prose: a label on the left saying what the
value is, the value on the right, the same shape every line.

```
पहले   HbA1c 8.2
अब     HbA1c 6.5     ← the only line carrying weight
समय    7 महीनों में
और     दवा आधी
```

The card used to print "HbA1c पहले" above "8.2 → 6.5" and then a bolded
fragment, so a reader met every number before being told what it meant and
three different bold things competed. A result told in plain words ("पहले
सीढ़ियाँ नहीं चढ़ पाते थे / अब रोज़ तीन मंज़िल") takes exactly the same shape,
so a row of cards reads as one thing.

No rules between the rows: four hairlines a card times six cards is a page of
lines, on a site whose repeated fault has been looking crowded. The labels
already say where one fact ends.
