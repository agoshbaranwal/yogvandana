Foundation 3 — “Ayurvedic”

The visual language the category already speaks in India: deep green for the practice and saffron for the call to act. Martel is a warm Devanagari serif with real weight range; Hind is the clean companion. Green does the serious work so saffron can stay bright without dominating — the two hold each other up instead of one shouting alone.

Reopens your round-1 decision of “no green”. Worth seeing before you keep it.
