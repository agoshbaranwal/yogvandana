Option 2 — Before and after, split down the middle

The success-stories section, redesigned. Same content, same fonts and colours as the live site. People and figures below are examples, not real students.

The card is literally divided into the problem and the solution. Left is ‘before’ in grey, right is ‘now’ in warm paper with the value in bold. An amber strip across the middle carries how long it took. Their words sit underneath with their face. This is the standard transformation layout of Indian health and fitness marketing, which this audience has already seen a hundred times.
