Option 3 — Their words are the ad

The success-stories section, redesigned. Same content, same fonts and colours as the live site. People and figures below are examples, not real students.

The person leads and their sentence is the headline. A large photograph, then a short line in their own words set at 23–27px as the headline of the card, then the fuller quote, then the numbers as a small outlined badge. The measurement is proof, not the pitch.

Why a marketer picks this: someone choosing a treatment is frightened, and a person is more persuasive than a statistic — this is how a premium health or therapy service sells. It also depends least on numbers she may not have. The risk: it needs real photographs and real quotes to work at all; with placeholders it looks emptier than the other two.
