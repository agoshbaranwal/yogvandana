Option 1 — The number is the ad

The success-stories section, redesigned. Same content, same fonts and colours as the live site. People and figures below are examples, not real students.

The result is the biggest thing on the card. A drop from 8.2 to 6.5 is set at 44–56px with an arrow between the two figures, the way a direct-response health advert does it. Everything else — the months, the medicine, the person — is support underneath a divider. A result told in plain words (“now three floors a day”) gets the same treatment at a slightly smaller size, so numeric and non-numeric stories look like one family.

Why a marketer picks this: the proof does the selling before anyone reads a word. On a phone you can take in the whole claim in under a second while scrolling. The risk: it leads with data, so it is colder — and it is weakest for the conditions where the result is not a number.
