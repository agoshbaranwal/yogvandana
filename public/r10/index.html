Success stories, redesigned — three options

Agosh: “a lot of sections are like this — not easily readable. Think how marketing people do it if they are selling something. Success stories is a part of our USP, it’s a part of the product.”

What is actually wrong today, measured. Nine of the site’s thirteen sections are flat: 70–95% of their text sits at 16–18px and the largest thing in them is 26px. The success-stories section is the worst of them — its biggest element is 20px, so nothing on it is louder than anything else and the eye has nowhere to land. That is not a contrast problem, which is what I fixed last time. It is an absence of hierarchy: no section is selling anything.

What all three options below share: one element per card is three to four times the size of the body text and carries the whole point, everything else supports it, and the section heading grows from 26px to 30–40px. They differ in what leads — the number, the transformation, or the person.
