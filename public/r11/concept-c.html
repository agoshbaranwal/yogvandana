Concept C — The slip is the product

A complete home page, in the site’s own fonts and colours. Names, figures and results below are examples so the layout can be judged — they are not real students.

Turn a service into an object. The prescription she writes after the consultation becomes the hero of the page: it is in the first screen, it is the second section, and its six rows are visibly locked. You can see exactly what you are buying and exactly what you do not get for free. An intangible ‘online yoga class’ is easy to postpone; a filled-in slip with your name on it is a thing you either have or do not have.
