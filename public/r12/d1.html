Direction 1 — The report

A complete home page. Names, figures and results are examples so the layout can be judged.

How it looks: White paper, hairline rules, no cards and no rounded corners. Numbered sections. Everything that can be a table is a table, with the figures aligned in a column. Headings are small; the density is high.

It looks like the lab report they are holding. This audience arrives with a printout — HbA1c, TSH, BP — and makes decisions from it. So the site is set as a record rather than as an advert: eight conditions in a table with a column of results, students shown as rows with a before, an after and a duration, the fees as a fee table, the slip as a form with nine lines. The selling is done by volume of checkable fact, and by the calm of a document that is not trying to persuade anyone.
