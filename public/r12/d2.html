Direction 2 — The poster

A complete home page. Names, figures and results are examples so the layout can be judged.

How it looks: Full-bleed colour fields — saffron, ink, ivory — with no container and no cards at all. Type at 46–132px. One idea per screen. Buttons are large pills.

Every screen is one statement you can read from across the room. The result is set at 132px on its own saffron field; the medicine answer gets a whole black screen to itself; the price list is three lines of 36px type. There are no cards because a card is a box that makes its contents smaller — here the content is the layout. The selling is done by confidence: a page this certain of itself reads as a business that is working.

Strongest for: a phone, a thumb, and four seconds. Unmissable when forwarded on WhatsApp, and it is what a modern Indian D2C brand looks like, which quietly signals that this is not one aunty with a Zoom link.
Weakest at: detail. It cannot carry nine facts about a batch or a full FAQ without breaking its own rule, so those pages must live somewhere quieter — and with placeholder content the big type has less to say.
